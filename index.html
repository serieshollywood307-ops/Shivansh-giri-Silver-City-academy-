About Us

Welcome to Silver City Academy

Located in Gola Gokarnath, Lakhimpur Kheri, Silver City Academy is dedicated to fostering an environment where students are inspired to reach their full potential.

Our school offers a dynamic and nurturing atmosphere where academic excellence, personal growth, and creativity come together. With passionate educators, state-of-the-art facilities, and a commitment to holistic learning, we equip students for success.

Our Premium Facilities

World-class infrastructure for holistic development

Transport Facilities

The school has its own vans for taking students to different places. School provides transport facility to students across the city ensuring total safety.

📚

Library

Our library is the most attractive place in school — designed to draw students naturally. Reading for enjoyment is key to educational success.

💻

Computer Labs

Computer education plays a vital role in career development. We prepare students for the digital age with modern computer labs.

🏏

Sports & Athletics

Football, volleyball, badminton, cricket — plus an annual Sports Day! Sports boost self-esteem, confidence and sportsmanship.

🎭

Extracurricular Activities

Music, sports, art, and cultural events. These activities enhance creativity, teamwork, and leadership skills for a well-rounded education.

🧪

Science Labs

Modern, well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs for hands-on learning and practical understanding of scientific concepts.
